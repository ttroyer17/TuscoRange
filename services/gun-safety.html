Gun Safety Classes

Building confident, responsible gun owners from day one.

Who This Class Is For

Our gun safety classes are designed specifically for new gun owners who want to start their firearms journey the right way. Whether you just purchased your first firearm or are considering it, this class gives you the foundation you need to handle, store, and operate your firearm safely and responsibly.

What You Will Learn

Safe Handling

Proper grip, stance, and the fundamental rules of firearm safety that every gun owner must know.

Safe Storage

How to store your firearm securely at home to prevent accidents and unauthorized access.

Responsible Ownership

Understanding your legal responsibilities as a gun owner and how to carry and transport your firearm legally.

What To Expect

Classes are held in a welcoming, judgment free environment. No prior experience is required — just a willingness to learn. Our instructors walk you through everything step by step, combining classroom instruction with hands on range time so you leave feeling confident and prepared.
