What Are Tactical Games?

Tactical Games are competitive shooting events that combine physical fitness with real world firearms skills. Competitors complete timed courses that test accuracy, speed, and tactical thinking — all in a safe, structured, and welcoming environment. Whether you are a seasoned shooter or picking up a firearm competitively for the first time, there is a place for you here.

What To Expect

Timed Shooting Courses

Compete on dynamic courses that challenge your accuracy and speed under realistic conditions.

Team Based Competition

Partner up or build a team and compete together. Great for building camaraderie and community.

Open To All Skill Levels

Beginners and experienced shooters compete in appropriate divisions. Everyone is welcome and everyone can improve.

Upcoming Events

Events are held regularly throughout the year. Contact us to get on our mailing list and be the first to know about upcoming competitions, registration deadlines, and special events.
